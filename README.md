# Lynx of Iberia

A turn-based conservation strategy game. You look after a family of Iberian lynx on the Iberian Peninsula. One turn is one season. Survive eight seasons.

**Play here:** open [index.html](index.html) in a browser, or use this preview:

https://htmlpreview.github.io/?https://github.com/fredpsantos-svg/lynx-of-iberia/blob/main/index.html

You do not control individual animals. Each season you spend three strategy points, then the land changes.
